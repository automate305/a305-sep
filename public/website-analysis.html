Top Recommendations
High Priority

Build a Lead Magnet Funnel

Create a free "Leadership Effectiveness Assessment" or "Exit Readiness Scorecard" that captures email addresses. Your blog gets traffic — convert it. This is the single highest-ROI change.

High Priority

Consolidate & Clarify Services

Reduce 6+ service pages to 3 clear offerings with distinct audiences. Each page should answer: "Who is this for? What do they get? What does it cost to start?" in the first scroll.

Medium Priority

Modernize the Platform

Audit WordPress for plugin bloat, run PageSpeed Insights, and consider whether a lighter platform (Webflow, Framer, or a modern WP theme) would better serve a brochure + blog site.

Medium Priority

Own Local SEO

Optimize for "executive coaching Miami," "family business consultant South Florida," and "business coach Aventura." Claim and optimize Google Business Profile. These are high-intent, low-competition terms.
